Hi, I'm Ricky 👋

An indie developer who believes good software should quietly make your day a little easier.

My Story

I've been writing code for years — first as a developer at a company, later growing into a tech lead role. After leaving that job, I spent a long stretch in e-commerce and social media, and achieved some success there. But in the end, I chose to come back to what I truly love: building products, this time as an independent developer.

Because no matter where I went, one thing stayed true — building things is what I love most.

Why I Build

I've never stayed inside the tech bubble — I'm curious about all kinds of industries and roles. That's the point: I want to use my development skills to help people solve the small, everyday problems in their work and life. Over the years I've built little tools for friends and small teams, and it's still the best part of what I do.

"Small tools, real problems, honest software."
